# Last Light

A lighthouse keeper's idle defence game for your phone. Keep the lamp burning from dusk till dawn while sea monsters close in, manage your oil so the light doesn't go dark, and spend your takings by day building up the harbour town that keeps you alive.

**Play it:** https://kralnu90.github.io/last-light/

## How it plays

- **One night per run.** Survive from dusk to dawn to move on to a harder night. Every third night, something big comes up from the deep.
- **Oil.** The lamp burns oil and monsters drop more. Set the lamp to Dim, Steady or Blaze to trade power against how long your oil lasts.
- **The town.** Build and upgrade on the cliffs between nights: oil stores, lens grinders, a cannon battery, a foghorn, a fishery and more. There are more buildings than plots, so choose wisely.

## Install

Open the game link on your phone and tap **Install app** on the title screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Once installed it runs full screen and works offline.

Your progress saves in your browser on the device you play on.
